## LIMITATION — this arm's instrument controls fired AFTER the measured invocation only

Recorded so a later citation cannot read A8's controls as before-and-after. The Batch H
design adopts a pre+post control chronology (pre-canary complete before PRODUCT-START,
post-canary after PRODUCT-COMPLETE, both content-bound in the ledger); THIS ARM PREDATES
THAT RULE and was deliberately not re-run.

WHAT THE CONTROL HERE DOES AND DOES NOT SHOW. Each case rewrites a declared harness probe
byte-identically after the measured invocation and requires the write witness to report it
(`witness_control_rewrite_seen=yes`, present in all four cases). That demonstrates the
witness and the change record were responsive AT THE AFTER-SNAPSHOT. It does not
demonstrate they were responsive DURING the invocation.

WHAT CARRIES THE BEFORE-HALF INSTEAD: the before-snapshot itself, taken before
PRODUCT-START, plus one property of the readings. A before-snapshot taken by a blind
instrument would be empty, and every path would then appear as ADDED in the after-diff.
The measured counts:
  a8-c01-fastpath-running-mutating   witness.before lines=33  manifest.before lines=33  added_paths=0  removed_paths=0  rewritten_paths_PRODUCT=0
  a8-c02-resume-stopped-mutating     witness.before lines=33  manifest.before lines=33  added_paths=0  removed_paths=0  rewritten_paths_PRODUCT=29
  a8-c03-inside-session-mutating     witness.before lines=33  manifest.before lines=33  added_paths=0  removed_paths=0  rewritten_paths_PRODUCT=0
  a8-c04-use-existing-mutating       witness.before lines=33  manifest.before lines=33  added_paths=0  removed_paths=0  rewritten_paths_PRODUCT=0

No case shows an empty before-snapshot and no case shows an all-added diff, so the specific
failure a pre-control guards against is excluded by the data this arm already holds. That
is a statement about this arm's own equipment; it is not a classification of anything the
product did, and it does not make the controls pre+post.

Ruling: seat ruling of 2026-08-20 — strengthen the rule going forward, disclose the
limitation here, do not re-run. Raised by the arm's own author.
