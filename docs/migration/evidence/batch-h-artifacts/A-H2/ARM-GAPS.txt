## What A-H2's five selector cases DO and DO NOT establish

ESTABLISHED: the refusal these five spellings produce, its exact bytes, its rc, and that
nothing was written under the fixture's AE_HOME.

NOT ESTABLISHED: whether `--detach` and `--no-attach` differ; whether the two selector
orders differ; whether a repeated selector differs. The config-presence check refuses
before any selector is consulted, so all five cases stop at the same place. Five spellings
with one reading here is NOT evidence that the spellings are equivalent — the question was
never asked.

WHAT WOULD LIFT IT: a fixture with a scaffolded steward config, so the invocations reach
the selector handling instead of the config check. That is a different fixture, not a
different reading of this one.

WHY IT IS RECORDED RATHER THAN FIXED IN PLACE: these cases establish a real thing. Rebuilt
in place they would establish a different thing and the first would be lost — the seat
disposition applied at A-H5's c09/c10 pair.
